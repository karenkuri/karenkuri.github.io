About Me

I'm Karen Hoffmann. My research focuses on how data visualization and interactive storytelling can make complex technological and societal topics more accessible, particularly in the context of technology forecasting and public engagement with the energy transition.

In my Master’s thesis at the University of Bremen in collaboration with DLR, I investigated storytelling approaches to data visualization for technology roadmaps, developing prototypes and studying how dynamic visualizations support understanding among academic, industry, and public-sector stakeholders. Related findings were presented as a conference paper at CHIIR 2025.

At the Center for Advanced Internet Studies (CAIS), I worked as a Data Scientist on a participatory citizen engagement project examining public perceptions and acceptance of sustainable urban infrastructure and green technologies including heat grids. I conducted qualitative and quantitative analysis (MAXQDA, R, Excel), organized user studies, and translated findings into publications, posters, and presentations, including at the IST 2025 conference.
